# LinkedIn Project Entry

**Project:** Healthcare Claims Payment & Revenue Cycle Analytics

Analyzed 50,000 healthcare claims using PostgreSQL and SQL to evaluate reimbursement performance, claim outcomes, provider/procedure patterns, payment turnaround, and data quality.

• Calculated $127.48M in expected reimbursement, $86.76M in paid reimbursement, and a $40.72M reimbursement gap.
• Analyzed 10,027 denied claims and identified denied reimbursement as a major area requiring further operational investigation.
• Investigated provider- and procedure-level denial patterns using claim volume, denial rate, and financial exposure.
• Calculated average, median, and maximum payment turnaround and investigated extreme delays.
• Found an average payment turnaround of 80.62 days versus a median of 28 days, indicating a long-tailed payment-time distribution.
• Performed SQL-based data-quality validation for duplicates, missing values, and financial inconsistencies.
• Documented analytical limitations and follow-up data requirements for deeper root-cause analysis.

**Tools:** PostgreSQL | SQL | Python/Pandas | Power BI | GitHub
